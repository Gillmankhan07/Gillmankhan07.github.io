Philosophy

How I think about building

I'm early in my engineering path, and I try to treat that honestly: I'm learning by building real things with real teams, not by claiming experience I don't have yet. On the fraud detection project, that meant focusing on the parts I could actually own — backend endpoints, query performance, and validating that the ML integration behaved as expected — rather than overstating my role on a five-person effort.

I care about systems being explainable as much as accurate. A risk score means nothing to an analyst if they can't see why it was assigned — that's part of what drew me to a project that paired ML scoring with human-readable reasoning.
